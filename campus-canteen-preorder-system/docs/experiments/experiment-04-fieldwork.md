# Experiment 4 — Requirement Elicitation Field Work

## Evidence boundary

No interview transcript, observation sheet, participant consent, survey response, or field date was supplied with the project template. The questions and forms below are a **fieldwork plan**. They are not findings. Complete them with real participants before describing needs as confirmed.

## Proposed participants and methods

| Participant group | Proposed method | Topic | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 students | Short semi-structured interview | Break length, queue, menu availability, preferred pickup/status | Anonymous notes, date, consent checkbox |
| 1–2 canteen workers | Interview plus process walkthrough | Order intake, preparation sequence, sold-out items, handover | Role-only notes; do not record personal data unnecessarily |
| 1 canteen manager | Interview | Menu ownership, daily reports, cancellation and peak-load rules | Confirmed policy notes and open questions |
| Students at one peak period | Non-intrusive observation, if approved | Queue steps, approximate wait intervals, order handover points | Time window, counting method, no photos of identifiable people |

Recruit participants only with instructor/canteen approval. Ask permission before taking notes or recording. Avoid collecting names, student IDs, payment information, or health information. Report anonymized roles and aggregate timings only.

## Interview guide

1. Please describe how you buy food during a short break today.
2. At which step do delays or confusion most often occur?
3. How do you learn that an item is unavailable?
4. What information would you need before placing an order?
5. How should pickup time, order changes, and cancellation work?
6. For staff: how are orders sequenced, prepared, and handed over during a rush?
7. What should happen if stock runs out after an order is placed?
8. What would make a pre-order system inconvenient or unusable?
9. Which information should students and staff be allowed to see or change?
10. May we use an anonymized summary of your feedback in our course record?

## Requirements to validate

The current prototype assumes a student can order only active and available items, staff progress orders one state at a time, students see only their own orders, and payment occurs at pickup. Ask participants whether these rules match actual canteen policy. Log each confirmation, disagreement, and requirement change with a requirement ID. Template-supplied quotes in Experiment 1 remain unverified until tied to a real session and consent record.
